New Zealand Primary Industries
Key Vocabulary
Before diving into the activities, review the key terms below. You will need these to answer the
questions in the following sections.
Biodiversity
The variety of plant and animal life in the world or in a
particular habitat, a high level of which is usually considered
important and desirable.
Horticulture
The branch of agriculture that deals with the art, science,
technology, and business of growing plants (fruits,
vegetables, flowers).
Pastoral Farming
Farming aimed at producing livestock (animals), such as dairy
cattle, beef cattle, and sheep, rather than growing crops.
Primary Production
The sector ofthe economy involved in the extraction and
production of raw materials, such as farming, logging,
hunting, fishing, and mining.
Ruminant
A hoofed mammal (like a cow or sheep) that chews the cud
and has a specialized stomach system to digest grass.
