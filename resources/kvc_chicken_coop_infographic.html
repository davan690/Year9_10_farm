CRITICAL SAFETY: AVIAN INFLUENZA

Immediate biosecurity actions required for all students.

Biosecurity is our first line of defense. Bird Flu (Avian Influenza) poses a significant risk to flock health. Students must monitor for symptoms daily. If illness is suspected, do not touch and evacuate the area immediately.

🚫

ZERO TOUCH POLICY: Never handle sick or dead birds.

🧼

HYGIENE: Wash hands with soap and warm water immediately after duty.

🧹 PERIODIC MAINTENANCE (5-6 WEEKS)

Nesting Box Deep Clean

Remove all old wood shavings. Transport old material directly to the School Compost Zone using a wheelbarrow.

WHEELBARROW COMPOST ZONE

Fresh Packing

Wipe down the interior of nesting boxes with dry cloth. Pack firmly with fresh, dry woodchips/shavings to ensure hen comfort.

Run Floor Audit

Inspect for wet/muddy patches. Spread sized woodchips across affected areas to prevent disease and maintain secure footing.

⚠️ Note: Report any loose mesh or ground gaps in perimeter fencing immediately.
