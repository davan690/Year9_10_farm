Practical Skills in Agriculture 
Testing for the Sugar Content of Food Using the Physics of Light  
 
Light is bent every time it comes through the lenses of our eyes; and every time it g oes 
through one substance like water or a ir, and then goes into another substance like glass or 
plastic. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The amount of dissolved substances in a solution bends light by different amounts. Sugar is a 
specific example of this. We can test how much sugar food has in it by seeing how much light 
can be bent by testing the juice of fruits and vegetables. 
Farmers can work out when their fruit is ripe - so they can pick it at just the right time to eat; 
or make wine from the fruit or preserve it in some way.  
A very common practical skill is to do this testing with a device called a refractometer. Below 
is a picture of one. The refractometer measures the amount of sugar in what are called Brix 
units. Higher values mean higher nutrition value.  
The procedure for doing the testing is shown on the right.
