# Autumn Planting Seedling Crop Guide

Use this guide when selecting crops for autumn sowing in Otago.

| Crop | Ideal germination temperature | Days to germinate | Days to maturity | Likely harvest window | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Broad beans | 10-18 C | 7-14 | 120-180 | September-October | Hardy. Best for overwintering and strong spring growth. |
| Spinach | 10-15 C | 7-12 | 40-60 | May-June | Reliable cool-season crop. Pick leaves as they grow. |
| Kale | 15-20 C | 5-10 | 60-80 | June-August | Very hardy. Often sweeter after frost. |
| Silverbeet | 15-20 C | 7-14 | 50-70 | June-September | Good beginner crop. Tolerates repeated picking. |
| Winter cabbage | 15-20 C | 7-12 | 90-120 | July-September | Needs planning and space. Best for later winter harvest. |
| Pak choy | 15-20 C | 5-10 | 45-60 | May-June | Fast crop but can struggle in frost or poor spacing. |
| Garlic | 10-15 C | 14-21 | 210-240 | December-January | Useful comparison crop. Slow, long-season, and planted for future harvest. |

## Decision prompts

- Will this crop cope with lower temperatures and shorter days?
- Is the expected harvest time realistic for the class calendar?
- Does it need frost protection?
- Is it better for leaf harvest, pod harvest, or a long-term comparison?

## Practical rule of thumb

- Autumn planting in Otago is often about getting roots established before winter rather than expecting quick top growth.
