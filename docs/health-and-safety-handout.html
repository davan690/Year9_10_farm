Student Farm Safety Handout

Farm Safety First

Before you work on the farm, remember: safety comes first. The farm is a real workplace, and the rules are there to keep you, other people, and the animals safe.

Essential rules

1. PPE

PPE means Personal Protective Equipment.

Use the correct PPE for the job:

  • gloves for heavy work, cutting, or plant handling
  • closed footwear for all farm work
  • hearing protection for loud machinery
  • any other protective gear required by the teacher

2. Safe movement

  • walk carefully and keep to marked or safe paths
  • keep clear of machinery and livestock
  • do not run in work areas
  • keep an escape route when working near animals

3. Tool and equipment safety

  • use tools only for their intended purpose
  • check tools before use
  • do not leave tools lying in walkways
  • clean and return them to storage after use

4. Animal safety

  • approach animals calmly and from a safe position
  • never surprise or corner an animal
  • do not stand in the flight path of bees
  • report injured or distressed animals to the teacher immediately

5. Chemical safety

If you are handling chemicals:

  • use the correct PPE
  • work in a supervised area
  • store chemicals in labelled, locked containers or sheds
  • keep a spill kit nearby
  • keep chemicals away from feed and water

Common hazards to look out for

  • quad bikes and vehicles losing control
  • machinery noise and vibration
  • chemicals and spills
  • livestock handling accidents
  • sharp tools and cutting equipment
  • slips, trips, and uneven ground
  • lifting injuries and heavy loads

Daily farm routine

Before and after each activity, check that:

  • tools are clean and stored correctly
  • walkways are clear
  • water troughs are clean and full
  • shelters and bedding are maintained
  • wood piles are tidy and safe
  • plants and tree guards are checked

Safety expectation

You are expected to behave responsibly and follow the farm safety rules at all times. If you are unsure about a task, stop, ask, and wait for instructions.

Safety is everyone’s responsibility.
