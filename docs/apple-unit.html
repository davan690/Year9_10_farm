Unit 5: Apple Unit (2 lessons)

Author

Urban Farm 2026

Published

Invalid Date

Purpose

This unit links practical orchard work with measurement and money maths. Students pick apples, clean fruit, weigh accurately, and pack fruit into sale bags.

Unit target for this year

  • Pack 4 x 2kg bags.
  • Price is $6 per 2kg bag.
  • Total target income: $24.
  • No 1kg bags this time (usually they are $4 per 1kg).

Learning goals

By the end of this unit, students can:

  • pick and handle fruit safely without bruising
  • clean and sort apples by quality
  • weigh fruit accurately to 2kg
  • pack, label, and count bags ready for sale
  • calculate expected income from packed bags

Lesson structure (2 lessons)

Lesson Focus Outcome
Lesson 1 Pick and clean fruit Apples are safely picked, cleaned, and sorted.
Lesson 2 Weigh, bag, and label fruit 4 x 2kg bags are packed and ready for sale.

Lesson 1: Picking and cleaning apples (40 min)

Goal: Harvest apples safely and prepare clean fruit for packing.

Student activities and instructions

  1. Put on gloves and check your picking area.
  2. Pick apples carefully (twist and lift, do not pull hard).
  3. Place fruit gently into crates/buckets (do not throw fruit).
  4. Remove leaves and obvious dirt.
  5. Wipe or rinse apples and place on drying trays.
  6. Sort into:
  • Sale quality (good skin, good size)
  • Second quality (bruised/small; not for 2kg sale bags)

Group tasks (20 students)

Group Students Task focus Key actions
Group A 7 Picking Pick apples safely and fill crates.
Group B 7 Cleaning Wipe/rinse apples and set to dry.
Group C 6 Sorting Sort apples into sale/second quality.

Timing (40 min)

  • 0-5 min: Safety briefing and tool/equipment check.
  • 5-25 min: Picking and crate collection.
  • 25-35 min: Cleaning and sorting.
  • 35-40 min: Clean-up and count prepared fruit.

Lesson 2: Weighing, bagging, and pricing (40 min)

Goal: Produce 4 x 2kg sale bags and calculate expected income.

Student activities and instructions

  1. Set up scales on flat ground and check zero.
  2. Fill each bag to 2.00kg (or as close as possible).
  3. Adjust weight by adding/removing apples.
  4. Seal each bag and label clearly:
  • “Apple Bag - 2kg”
  • Price “$6”
  1. Repeat until 4 bags are complete.
  2. Calculate income:
  • \(4 \times 6 = 24\)
  1. Record final numbers in class sheet.

Timing (40 min)

  • 0-5 min: Scale setup and demo.
  • 5-25 min: Weigh and pack 4 bags.
  • 25-35 min: Label, check quality, and count stock.
  • 35-40 min: Income calculation and tidy up.

Tools and materials

  • picking buckets/crates
  • gloves
  • clean cloths or rinse tubs
  • digital or kitchen scales
  • packing bags
  • labels/marker pens
  • cleaning spray/paper towels for workstations

Health and safety guidelines

Risk Guideline/control
Slips/trips in orchard Walk, do not run; keep paths clear of crates and bags.
Ladder/fall risk Only teacher-approved students use ladders; 3-point contact at all times.
Cuts/scratches Wear gloves; handle branches carefully; report injuries immediately.
Food hygiene risk Clean hands before handling sale fruit; use clean cloths and clean surfaces.
Manual handling strain Lift crates with bent knees; share heavy loads.
Bee/wasp stings Stay calm near insects; notify teacher if allergic reaction risk.
Sun exposure/dehydration Hats, sunscreen, and water breaks required.

Teacher checks (end of unit)

Reflection

  • What helped your group weigh bags accurately?
  • What quality issues did you notice most often?
  • If we used 1kg bags next time, how would sales options change?
