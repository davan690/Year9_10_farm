Unit 5: Organisation and Inventory

Keeping the farm workspace safe, tidy, and efficient

Author

Urban Farm 2026

Published

August 15, 2026

Overview

This unit focuses on the importance of organisation and good housekeeping on the farm. A tidy and well-managed workspace improves safety, saves time, and makes tools and equipment easier to find when they are needed.

Why this matters

A farm is a busy workplace. If tools are left lying around, materials are poorly stored, or tasks are not logged, it becomes harder to work safely and efficiently. Good organisation protects people, equipment, and the wider farm environment.

Learning goals

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:

  • explain why tidy storage matters for safety;
  • organise tools, pots, and materials in a practical way;
  • maintain a safe and accessible workspace;
  • complete a basic equipment check;
  • record issues or missing items clearly.

Equipment

  • broom or brush
  • gloves
  • notebook or checklist
  • labels or marker if available

Before you start

  • Walk through the area first and identify hazards.
  • Keep walkways clear.
  • Handle heavy items carefully.
  • Report broken equipment or obvious safety risks.

Organisation principles

  • A place for everything and everything in its place.
  • Most-used items should be easiest to reach.
  • Heavy items should be stored lower down.
  • Label storage areas clearly.
  • Keep walkways and exits clear.

Inventory note

Reflection

  1. Why does good organisation make a farm safer?
  2. What happens when tools are left out and not returned to storage?
  3. How could a tidy workspace improve productivity and care of equipment?

Extension

Create a simple inventory checklist for the farm that could be used at the start of each term.
